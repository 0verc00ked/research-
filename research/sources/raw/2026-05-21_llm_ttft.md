## On reasoning variants (avoid for order-path latency budget)
- GPT-5 mini (high reasoning): TTFT ~67 s
- GPT-5.4 mini (medium): 10.7 s
- GPT-5.4 mini (xhigh): 5.2 s
- Claude Haiku 4.5 reasoning + Gemini 2.5 Flash reasoning: similarly higher than non-reasoning

## Stated SLA vs observed
- Anthropic, Google, OpenAI publish no TTFT SLA. All numbers are observed benchmarks (single-tenant, lab conditions).
- Production p99 will be materially worse; one third-party (kunalganglani.com 2026 benchmark) cites Haiku 4.5 p95 at ~612 ms and total response p99 in seconds.

## Implication for design
For a 5-min cadence analyst loop, all three non-reasoning models are well inside budget (<2 s TTFT). Reasoning variants are unusable in the order path or any sub-1-min loop. Pin the analyst to non-reasoning tier; reserve reasoning for offline overnight research jobs.

STALENESS: HIGH — TTFT numbers move with provider load and capacity; re-benchmark per-quarter at minimum.
