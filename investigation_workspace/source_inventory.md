## Credibility Assessment

The repository reports are useful as search scaffolding but should not be treated as evidence databases. They repeatedly use phrases such as "takeover," "corruption," "systematic penetration," and "extractive by design" before proving causal chains. Several local claims are numerically outdated or overstated when checked against official 2026 releases.

Most reliable verified facts in the current package come from official NHA, CCI/PIB, company filings/releases, and peer-reviewed literature. The weakest areas are India-specific causal claims linking PE ownership to pricing, patient harm, food prices, farmer bargaining power, and regulatory capture.
