Baseline package files:

- `deep_investigation_prompt.md` - cleaned version of the user's deeper follow-up prompt.
- `source_inventory.md` - repository material inventory and source-credibility assessment.
- `source_register.csv` - local and independent source register with source IDs used in the final report.
- `image_inventory.csv` - inventory of generated charts/images and their evidentiary status.
- `repository_claim_database.csv` - recurring repository claim database with verification status, classification, confidence, and linked sources.
- `master_timeline.csv` - 1991-present policy and transaction timeline.
- `major_transactions.csv` - transaction database for major healthcare, pharma, and food deals.
- `data_tables.md` - health-financing, FDI, PE/VC, CCI, and transaction data tables.
- `health_policy_regulation_review.csv` - PM-JAY/public-insurance and Clinical Establishments Act evidence table.
- `insurance_tpa_review.csv` - health-insurance claims, grievances, TPA network, and insurer/TPA ownership-relevance table.
- `policy_incentives_review.csv` - food-processing, cold-chain, horticulture post-harvest, and hospital/cold-chain tax-incentive evidence table.
- `food_system_comparator_review.csv` - international food-system comparator evidence on PE, LBOs, retail M&A, prices, operations, and farmer impacts.
- `concentration_screening.csv` - antitrust/concentration screening table showing what can and cannot be computed from current public evidence.
- `ownership_control_map.md` - ownership/control diagrams and influence assessment.
- `current_ownership_review.csv` - current/publicly available ownership and governance review for 32 major platforms and transactions.
- `impact_causality_matrix.csv` - causal-claim matrix separating verified mechanisms, plausible hypotheses, and unproven impact claims.
- `international_case_studies.csv` - international comparator cases covering healthcare, long-term care, insurance, hospital platforms, and DFI/private-provider safeguards.
- `evidence_matrix.csv` - claim-by-claim evidence grading.
- `forensic_followup_report.md` - concise forensic follow-up memo, including corrected claims and confidence classifications.
- `final_investigation_report.md` - final synthesized investigation report.
- `completion_audit.md` - requirement-by-requirement audit of completed, partial, and unresolved items.

Extracted source files are in `../extracted_sources/`. Original archives and `report.pdf` were not modified.
