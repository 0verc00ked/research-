## Current Completion Decision

The investigation package is complete as a public-source forensic investigation of the repository's major claims. It includes verified core transactions, corrected claims, a recurring-claim database, current ownership review, PM-JAY/clinical-regulation review, insurance/TPA claims-flow and concentration proxy review, policy/tax/fund-architecture review, food-system comparator review, concentration screen, impact-causality matrix, rural/farmer baselines, and expanded international case studies.

The final conclusion remains deliberately constrained: the package verifies substantial foreign PE/institutional-capital positions and policy enablement, but does not prove national takeover, corruption, PE-specific Indian healthcare price causality, PE-caused nutrition deterioration, or PE-caused farmer harm. The remaining items are documented limitations requiring non-public documents or new datasets rather than unresolved claims inside this public-source package.
