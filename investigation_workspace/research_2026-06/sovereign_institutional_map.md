## 1. Overview

This dossier maps long-horizon, government-linked and pension capital — sovereign wealth funds (SWFs), public pension plans, and quasi-sovereign holding companies — across India's healthcare, pharmaceutical, food and agriculture systems. Unlike classic buyout PE (Blackstone, KKR, Bain, TPG, Carlyle — covered in sibling cells), these investors are characterised by: (a) very long holding periods, (b) state or pensioner beneficiaries, (c) a willingness to take both control and minority positions, and (d) a strong preference for "defensive growth" sectors — exactly hospitals, single-specialty care, branded food, and seeds/agri-inputs.

**Headline finding (STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION):** A small cluster of foreign sovereign/pension funds now sits at or near the top of India's largest private hospital and branded-food assets. The single most consequential position is **Temasek (Singapore)**, which controls **Manipal Health Enterprises** — India's second-largest hospital chain — with a ~59% stake, and which simultaneously holds positions in Medanta, Dr Agarwal's, Cloudnine, Haldiram's, Country Delight, Licious and Rebel Foods. **GIC (Singapore)** controls/anchors **Asia Healthcare Holdings** (Motherhood, Nova IVF, AINU). **ADIA, Mubadala (Abu Dhabi), Novo Holdings (Denmark) and CalPERS (US)** are co-investors in Manipal. **Khazanah (Malaysia)** is the largest disclosed shareholder bloc behind **IHH Healthcare**, which in turn is the strategic owner of **Fortis** in India, and Khazanah separately owns ~15% of **Wow! Momo**. **OTPP (Canada)** owned and then sold **Sahyadri** — to Temasek's Manipal — a clean illustration of consolidation. **Saudi SALIC** (a PIF subsidiary) holds ~9.2% of **LT Foods** (Daawat basmati). **Alpha Wave Global** and Abu Dhabi's **IHC** sit in **Haldiram's** and **Advanta** (seeds).

**Concentration signal (EXPERT INTERPRETATION):** Singapore state capital (Temasek + GIC) alone has controlling or near-controlling positions across India's #2 hospital chain (Manipal) and a leading single-specialty platform (AHH), plus the country's largest branded-snacks company (Haldiram's, 10%). Gulf sovereign capital (ADIA, Mubadala, QIA, IHC, SALIC) is the fastest-growing second cluster. Canadian pensions (OTPP, CPPIB, CDPQ) and US public pension (CalPERS) are present but mostly via minority/credit positions — with OTPP's Sahyadri being the notable control exception, now exited.

## 2. Detailed findings by fund

### 2.1 Temasek (Singapore) — the dominant position

Temasek is the single largest sovereign player in Indian healthcare and a rising one in food. Its India head Ravi Lambah stated in **July 2024** that Temasek sees potential to deploy a further **up to US$10 billion in India over the next three years**, with healthcare a named theme (alongside financial services, consumption, digitisation, sustainability), citing Manipal, Medanta and Dr Agarwal's as anchors and putting total India exposure on a path toward ~US$47bn. [VERIFIED FACT — investor statement] (https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/temasek-looks-to-invest-10-billion-in-the-next-3-years-in-india-124071500913_1.html ; https://www.cnbc.com/2024/07/16/temasek-aims-to-invest-up-to-10-billion-in-india-as-china-weighs.html)

**Manipal Health Enterprises (MHE) — CONTROL.** On **10 April 2023**, Temasek signed definitive agreements to acquire an additional **41%** of MHE, taking it to a controlling position; post-deal the Manipal/Pai group would hold ~30%, TPG (via TPG Asia VIII) ~11%, and Temasek's existing Sheares Healthcare ~18% — and **NIIF (India's quasi-sovereign fund) exited completely**. [VERIFIED FACT — Temasek/NIIF release] (https://niifindia.in/news/niif-to-exit-its-investment-from-manipal-health-enterprises/). Press reported the 41% tranche at ~US$2bn and an enterprise value of roughly ₹40,000–42,000 crore, with Temasek's total stake reaching **59%**. [STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION — press-reported value] (https://www.bwhealthcareworld.com/article/singapores-temasek-acquires-majority-stake-in-manipal-hospitals-for-2-billion-472147). Manipal is described in the Mubadala release as **India's second-largest hospital chain by beds**, ~30+ hospitals, ~6 million patients/year, 5,000+ doctors. [VERIFIED FACT] (https://www.mubadala.com/en/news/mubadala-invests-in-india-manipal-health-enterprises)

In **February 2024**, Temasek **sold ~8% of MHE** to a consortium of **Mubadala (Abu Dhabi), Novo Holdings (Denmark) and CalPERS (US)**; Temasek retained its majority (~59% pre-sale stated; the 8% came out of the broader cap table). The split among the three buyers was **not disclosed**; deal value undisclosed. Post-deal Pai family ~30%, TPG ~11%. [VERIFIED FACT that the three funds bought 8% from Temasek] (https://www.agbi.com/people-lifestyle/2024/02/mubadala-buys-stake-in-indian-hospital-chain/ ; https://www.mubadala.com/en/news/mubadala-invests-in-india-manipal-health-enterprises)

**Sahyadri (via Manipal) — CONTROL chain.** In **July 2025**, Manipal (Temasek-controlled) agreed to **buy Sahyadri Hospitals from OTPP** at ~₹5,800 crore, taking Manipal to 49 hospitals. [STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION — press-reported value; transaction confirmed] (https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/manipal-hospitals-acquires-sahyadri-ontario-teachers-exits-with-2-5x-125071000169_1.html). This is the clearest single example of sovereign-capital-driven consolidation: a Canadian pension's control asset folded into a Singapore-controlled chain.

**Medanta / Global Health Ltd — MINORITY (listed).** Temasek holds **~17%** in Global Health (Medanta), held via **Dunearn Investments Mauritius Pte Ltd** — a Mauritius vehicle — as reflected in the company's pre/post-IPO shareholding; Temasek bought into Global Health in **January 2022**. Promoter Naresh Trehan ~35%; Carlyle's Anant Investments (~25.67%) exited via the Nov-2022 IPO. [STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION — shareholding-pattern reported; Mauritius vehicle named] (https://www.business-standard.com/article/markets/medanta-ipo-co-founder-sunil-sachdeva-and-carlyle-to-offload-stake-121100101030_1.html)

**Dr Agarwal's Health Care — MINORITY (pre-IPO/listed).** As of the DRHP, **Temasek held 28.18%** and TPG 33.75%, promoters 37.83%. SEBI approved the IPO (₹300cr fresh + OFS up to 69.57m shares) on **8 Jan 2025** (DRHP filed 27 Sep 2024); the eye-care chain listed in early 2025. [VERIFIED FACT — DRHP-derived stakes] (https://ipocentral.in/dr-agarwal-health-care-gets-sebi-approval/)

**Cloudnine (mother & baby care) — MINORITY (via Sheares).** Reported (Sep 2024) to be acquiring **18–20%** from Peak XV at a ~**US$600m** valuation, plus a ~₹359cr primary round (Temasek + TPG NewQuest). Earlier (Feb 2023) Temasek was in talks for 15–20% at a ₹30bn (~US$363m) valuation via Sheares. [PLAUSIBLE HYPOTHESIS / press-reported — deal terms in negotiation; not in a filing] (https://medicalbuyer.co.in/temasek-likely-to-buy-peak-xvs-18-20-stake-in-cloudnine-hospitals/ ; https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/temasek-in-talks-to-buy-stake-in-india-s-cloudnine-hospital-chain-report-123021600323_1.html)

**HCG (HealthCare Global) — DID NOT PROCEED.** Temasek was reported (May 2024) to be among bidders for CVC's ~60% of cancer-care chain HCG, but the controlling stake ultimately went to **KKR** (Feb 2025, up to 54% at ₹445/share, ~US$400m). Recorded here as a near-miss to avoid overstating Temasek's footprint. [VERIFIED FACT — KKR release] (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250223150894/en/KKR-Acquires-Controlling-Stake-in-Indian-Healthcare-Provider-Healthcare-Global-Enterprises-for-$400-Million)

**Food & agri — MINORITY positions:**

[STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION — press-reported stake/value; PwC confirmed the strategic-investment fact] (https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/haldirams-hits-10-billion-valuation-as-ihc-alpha-wave-join-temasek-in-back-to-back-deals-470046-2025-03-31 ; https://www.pwc.in/press-releases/2025/haldirams-attracts-strategic-investment-via-alpha-wave-global-and-ihc.html)

[STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION — press-reported] (https://yourstory.com/2024/01/country-delight-temasek-venturi-partners-funding-valuation-dairy-startup ; https://inc42.com/buzz/country-delight-to-net-25-mn-from-temasek/)
- **Licious (D2C meat/seafood)** — Temasek led the **US$192m Series F (Jul 2021)** at a ~US$650m valuation (with Multiples PE, Brunei Investment Agency); Temasek-backed and preparing a 2026 IPO. [STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION — press-reported] (https://inc42.com/buzz/licious-raises-192-mn-in-series-f-round-led-by-temasek-others/ ; https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-02-18/temasek-backed-indian-meat-seller-seeks-2-billion-ipo-valuation)
- **Rebel Foods (cloud kitchens)** — Temasek led a **~US$210m pre-IPO/Series G round (Dec 2024)**; sought CCI approval Oct 2024 for a US$100–150m investment. [STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION — press-reported + CCI-filing fact] (https://yourstory.com/2024/12/rebel-foods-210m-ipo-round-temasek-cloud-kitchen ; https://www.business-standard.com/companies/start-ups/temasek-seeks-cci-approval-to-buy-stake-in-foodtech-firm-rebel-foods-124101601238_1.html)
- **Devyani International (KFC/Pizza Hut/Costa operator)** — Temasek invested ~₹500cr (~US$82m) in **Oct 2014**, including buying part of ICICI Venture's holding. [VERIFIED FACT — historical; press-reported] (https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/singapore-temasek-to-kfc-devyani-icici-venture-icici-bank-141064-2014-10-09)

[VERIFIED FACT — company/PRNewswire release; historical] (https://www.prnewswire.com/in/news-releases/intas-pharmaceuticals-announces-completion-of-the-sale-of-chryscapitals-1016-stake-to-temasek-282122721.html)

### 2.2 GIC (Singapore) — single-specialty control via AHH

**Asia Healthcare Holdings (AHH) — CONTROL/anchor.** GIC invested **US$170m in Feb 2022** (reported minority of ~40–45%) and a **further US$150m in Dec 2024**, total **US$320m**, in TPG-backed AHH. Press reported GIC moving to a majority/larger position with the 2024 round, valuing AHH at ~US$800–900m. AHH owns **Motherhood Hospitals** (24 women & children's hospitals, 12 cities), **Nova IVF Fertility** (~95 centres, 57 cities), and **Asian Institute of Nephrology & Urology (AINU)**. [VERIFIED FACT — GIC/TPG release for amounts/portfolio; majority-status is press-reported] (https://www.gic.com.sg/newsroom/all/gic-and-tpg-backed-asia-healthcare-holdings-announce-further-us150-million-investment-from-gic/ ; https://www.equentis.com/blog/singapore-gics-15-stake-expansion-in-asia-healthcare-holdings-pegs-valuation-at-800-900-million/)

**Rainbow Children's Medicare — anchor/minority (listed).** Government of Singapore + Monetary Authority of Singapore were among the **36 anchor investors** raising ₹469.55cr in the **April 2022 IPO**. [VERIFIED FACT — anchor allocation] (https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/rainbow-medicare-raises-rs-470-cr-from-anchor-investors-ahead-of-ipo-122042601245_1.html). (GIC-branded entities frequently appear under "Government of Singapore" in Indian shareholding patterns.)

**VeGrow (agritech/B2B fruit & veg) — MINORITY.** GIC reportedly led a Series C in VeGrow (company has raised ~US$86.5m total). [PLAUSIBLE HYPOTHESIS — single-source, press-reported; the "GIC" attribution should be confirmed against the primary round announcement] (https://theweeklynews.in/top-10-agritech-startups-in-india/)

### 2.4 Mubadala (Abu Dhabi) — Manipal co-investor

Mubadala states it has invested ~US$4bn in India and calls it a priority market. [VERIFIED FACT — Mubadala release] (https://www.mubadala.com/en/news/mubadala-invests-in-india-manipal-health-enterprises ; https://www.agbi.com/people-lifestyle/2024/02/mubadala-buys-stake-in-indian-hospital-chain/)

[VERIFIED FACT that no QIA-Cloudnine link surfaced; treat "QIA-Cloudnine" as UNPROVEN CLAIM] (https://medicalbuyer.co.in/temasek-likely-to-buy-peak-xvs-18-20-stake-in-cloudnine-hospitals/)

### 2.7 Canadian pensions — OTPP, CPPIB, CDPQ

**OTPP (Ontario Teachers') — Sahyadri: CONTROL, then EXIT.** Bought a **significant majority** of Sahyadri Hospitals (largest private chain in Maharashtra) from Everstone in **Aug 2022** (its first India buyout; Everstone-era valuation ~₹2,500cr). **Sold to Manipal in 2025** at ~₹5,800cr, ~2.5x return; Sahyadri had grown to 11 hospitals / 1,400+ beds. [VERIFIED FACT — OTPP/Alvarez & Marsal/PRNewswire releases] (https://www.prnewswire.com/in/news-releases/ontario-teachers-announces-agreement-to-acquire-a-significant-majority-stake-in-sahyadri-hospitals-from-the-everstone-group-890812433.html ; https://www.otpp.com/en-ca/about-us/news-and-insights/2025/ontario-teachers-announces-sale-of-sahyadri-hospitals/)

**CPPIB (CPP Investments) — present, no confirmed direct hospital/pharma control.** Has committed >US$2bn to India since 2010 (Mumbai office); named India holdings (Kotak Mahindra Bank, Byju's, Delhivery, Eruditus, SBI Life, India Resurgence Fund) skew financial/consumer; **no specific Manipal/Eris/Radiant healthcare control position was confirmed** in this research. [STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION for presence; the specific health/pharma targets in the brief are UNPROVEN as direct CPPIB holdings] (https://www.cppinvestments.com/newsroom/cppib-india-office/)

**CDPQ (La Caisse) — credit-led healthcare exposure.** US$4.5bn+ invested in India since 2016. Healthcare exposure is largely **indirect/credit**: a **US$300m private-credit platform with Piramal** (CDPQ 75%) targeting healthcare/pharma/manufacturing; CDPQ also took a minority in **TVS Logistics** (serves healthcare/FMCG, >₹1,000cr / ~US$155m). No confirmed direct hospital/pharma equity control found. [VERIFIED FACT — La Caisse releases] (https://www.lacaisse.com/en/news/pressreleases/cdpq-piramal-partner-deploy-us300-m-private-credit-financing-india ; https://www.lacaisse.com/en/news/pressreleases/cdpq-acquires-substantial-minority-interest-tvs-logistics-services)

### 2.9 Saudi PIF / SALIC — agriculture & rice

**SALIC (PIF subsidiary) — LT Foods (Daawat basmati): from subsidiary stake to listed parent.** SALIC originally took **29.91%/29.5% of Daawat Foods** (an LT Foods manufacturing subsidiary, ~22% of group output) from India Agri Business Fund in **2020** (buyback valued at ~₹176cr / ~US$21.7m). In the restructuring (reported Jan 2025), **LT Foods bought back SALIC's Daawat stake**, and **SALIC instead took ~9.22% directly in listed LT Foods** for **~SAR208m / US$55.4m** (7.89% from the company + 1.33% from existing owners), plus a basmati supply/co-operation agreement for Saudi/Middle-East markets. [VERIFIED FACT — just-food/Gulf News + LT Foods disclosures] (https://www.just-food.com/news/indias-lt-foods-buys-back-daawat-foods-stake-in-salic-saudi-arabia-partnership/ ; https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/salic-acquires-2991-per-cent-stake-in-indias-daawat-foods-1.71666322)

**PIF (direct) — Jio Platforms (telecom, out of sector).** ~2.32% for US$1.5bn (2020); listed only to show PIF's India scale; no confirmed direct PIF healthcare/pharma/food equity beyond SALIC's agri positions. [VERIFIED FACT] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Investment_Fund)

### 2.10 Abu Dhabi IHC + Alpha Wave Global — Haldiram's & Advanta (seeds)

**Haldiram's — MINORITY.** **IHC (International Holding Company, Abu Dhabi) + Alpha Wave Global** together took **~6%** of Haldiram Snacks Food at a ~**US$10bn (~₹85,000cr)** valuation (Mar 2025), alongside Temasek's ~10%. [STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION — press-reported; PwC advisory confirmation] (https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/after-temasek-haldiram-s-attracts-two-more-investors-ihc-alpha-wave-125033100747_1.html ; https://www.pwc.in/press-releases/2025/haldirams-attracts-strategic-investment-via-alpha-wave-global-and-ihc.html)

**Advanta Enterprises (UPL's global seed company) — MINORITY (agri-inputs).** **Alpha Wave Global** agreed (**19 Nov 2024**) to invest **US$350m for ~12.5%** of Advanta (US$100m primary + US$250m secondary from UPL; UPL used proceeds to deleverage). This was the **second global investor after KKR's US$300m (Oct 2022)**. [VERIFIED FACT — UPL release / Indian Chemical News] (https://www.indianchemicalnews.com/general/upl-to-sell-125-stake-in-advanta-enterprises-to-alpha-wave-global-for-us-350-mn-24166 ; https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/upl-shares-rally-after-alpha-wave-global-buys-12-stake-in-subsidiary-124112100535_1.html). *Note: Alpha Wave is a global PE/credit manager, not strictly a sovereign fund, but is closely associated with Abu Dhabi capital (Alpha Wave Ventures backers include Mubadala/CHIMERA-linked capital); classified here as institutional.*

## 4. Concentration & cross-cutting analysis

**4.1 Largest sovereign/pension footprints in Indian health/food (EXPERT INTERPRETATION, evidence-based):**

1. **Temasek (Singapore)** — by far the deepest. *Control:* Manipal (~59%, #2 hospital chain) and, through Manipal, Sahyadri. *Significant minority:* Medanta (~17%), Dr Agarwal's (~28%), Cloudnine (~18–20% reported), Haldiram's (~10%), plus Country Delight, Licious, Rebel Foods, Devyani. Temasek alone spans hospitals + single-specialty + branded food + D2C dairy/meat. This is the single most concentrated sovereign position in the dataset.
2. **GIC (Singapore)** — controls/anchors AHH (Motherhood + Nova IVF + AINU), the leading multi-vertical single-specialty platform; plus Rainbow anchor. Singapore state capital (Temasek + GIC) therefore touches India's #2 hospital chain, a leading single-specialty platform, pediatric care, and the #1 branded-snacks company.
3. **Khazanah (Malaysia)** — anchors IHH (→ Fortis ~31%, Gleneagles), one of India's largest hospital groups, plus Wow! Momo (~15%).
4. **Abu Dhabi cluster (ADIA, Mubadala, IHC)** — Mubadala in Manipal; ADIA in Rainbow + Sapphire Foods; IHC in Haldiram's. Fast-growing, currently minority.
5. **Canadian pensions (OTPP, CPPIB, CDPQ)** — OTPP's Sahyadri was the standout control position (now exited to Temasek); CPPIB/CDPQ are mostly minority/credit.
6. **CalPERS (US)** — Manipal co-investor + Zepto lead; small but notable as direct US public-pension money in Indian hospitals and grocery delivery.
7. **Saudi (SALIC/PIF)** — agri/rice via LT Foods (~9.2%).

**4.2 Control vs minority (VERIFIED/STRONG):**
- **CONTROL positions:** Temasek → Manipal (~59%) and Sahyadri (via Manipal); GIC → AHH (majority per press); Khazanah → IHH (largest bloc, control-influencing) → Fortis strategic associate (~31%); OTPP → Sahyadri (majority, until 2025 exit).
- **MINORITY positions:** Temasek (Medanta, Dr Agarwal's, Cloudnine, Haldiram's, food D2C); Mubadala/Novo/CalPERS (Manipal 8%); ADIA (Rainbow ~2%, Sapphire Foods); IHC+Alpha Wave (Haldiram's ~6%, Advanta ~12.5%); SALIC (LT Foods ~9.2%); CalPERS (Zepto); QIA (Swiggy).

**4.3 Consolidation vector (EXPERT INTERPRETATION):** The OTPP→Manipal(Temasek) Sahyadri sale and Manipal's broader roll-up (49 hospitals) show sovereign capital not merely buying assets but **rolling up regional chains under a single Singapore-controlled platform**. Combined with GIC's AHH single-specialty platform and Khazanah/IHH's Fortis, a meaningful slice of India's organised private hospital capacity now sits under four foreign government-linked owners. The food side shows a parallel pattern: Haldiram's (#1 snacks) now has Temasek + IHC + Alpha Wave on its cap table at a US$10bn valuation.

**4.4 Vehicle/jurisdiction signal (STRONG, where disclosed):** Confirmed offshore structuring includes **Temasek's Medanta stake via Dunearn Investments Mauritius Pte Ltd** (Mauritius) and Temasek's hospital investing arm **Sheares Healthcare (Singapore)**. Most Singapore SWF flows route via Singapore/Mauritius entities; Gulf funds invest as ADIA/Mubadala/IHC/SALIC directly or via UAE/KSA vehicles; CalPERS/OTPP/CPPIB/CDPQ via their own Canadian/US vehicles or India-registered FPIs.
