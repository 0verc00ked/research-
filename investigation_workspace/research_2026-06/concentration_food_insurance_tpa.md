# Market Concentration: Packaged-Food Categories, Health Insurance, and TPAs in India

**Research cell:** Concentration estimates — (1) packaged food categories (salty snacks/namkeen, biscuits, chocolate); (2) health insurance (standalone health insurers + general insurers' health books, with HHI/CR4 and foreign JV mapping); (3) Third-Party Administrators (TPA).
**Compiled:** 2026-06-24
**Scope of capital lens:** foreign PE / sovereign-wealth (Temasek, ADIA, IHC) / pension / institutional capital and foreign strategic JV partners (Bupa, Cigna, Allianz, Novo Holdings), with Indian PE noted where it interacts.

---

## 0. Evidence-classification key

Every numeric claim below is tagged with one of:

- **VERIFIED FACT** — from a regulator filing (SEBI DRHP/RHP, IRDAI, CCI order), official company release, or a regulator-attributed dataset.
- **STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION** — derived/computed by the analyst from VERIFIED FACT inputs (e.g., HHI/CR4 computed from disclosed shares).
- **EXPERT INTERPRETATION** — analyst/brokerage estimate presented as such by a reputable research house.
- **PLAUSIBLE HYPOTHESIS** — directionally supported but not independently confirmed.
- **UNPROVEN CLAIM** — single-source/press assertion not corroborated.

**Critical caveat on "market share" definitions:** In packaged food, shares swing wildly with the denominator (total savoury-snacks market vs *organized* market vs *traditional/ethnic* namkeen sub-segment). The same company (Haldiram) is ~13% of one denominator and ~38.5% of another. Every food share below states its denominator explicitly. Treat cross-source comparison with caution.
