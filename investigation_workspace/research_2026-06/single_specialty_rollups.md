# Single-Specialty PE Roll-Ups in Indian Healthcare — Evidence Dossier

**Research cell:** Single-specialty PE roll-ups (eye, fertility, mother & child, dialysis, oncology, nephro-urology)
**Investigation:** "Foreign Private Equity and Institutional Capital in India's Healthcare, Pharmaceutical, Food and Agriculture Systems"
**Compiled:** 2026-06-24
**Analyst note:** Every numeric claim carries a source URL and an evidence classification. Classifications used: **VERIFIED FACT** (in a filing, regulator order, or first-party company/investor release), **STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION**, **EXPERT INTERPRETATION**, **PLAUSIBLE HYPOTHESIS**, **UNPROVEN CLAIM**. Deal values that appear only in financial press and not in a filing/first-party release are explicitly marked **press-reported**. USD/INR conversions are approximate and use period-appropriate rates (~INR 75–87/USD over 2019–2025); treat conversions as indicative, not official.

## 1. Overview

Single-specialty hospital chains — focused on one clinical vertical (eyes, fertility/IVF, mother & child, dialysis, oncology, nephro-urology) — have become the most concentrated arena of foreign private-equity, sovereign-wealth, and development-finance roll-up activity in Indian healthcare. The economic logic is well documented in first-party investor releases: a "doctor-led," asset-light, replicable unit (an eye hospital, an IVF lab, a dialysis bay) can be cloned across tier-2/tier-3 cities, bolt-on M&A consolidates fragmented owner-operators, and the consolidated platform is then exited via IPO or a secondary sale to a larger fund.

Key structural findings (each detailed and sourced below):

- **Eye care** is led by **Dr Agarwal's Health Care** (TPG + Temasek controlled pre-IPO; IPO Feb 2025) and **ASG Eye Hospitals** (General Atlantic + Kedaara, ~46% in 2022, after an Investcorp exit). **Centre for Sight** passed from Mahindra to **ChrysCapital** (2024). **Maxivision** took up to ₹1,300 cr from **Quadria Capital** (2023). **Eye-Q** is venture-backed (Nexus, Helion, HOYA). Foreign-PE control is concentrated in Dr Agarwal's and ASG.
- **Fertility/IVF** is the most heavily consolidated vertical. **Indira IVF** (market leader) was acquired to a **majority/control stake by BPEA EQT** in 2023 (press-reported ~60% for ~₹6,000 cr, valuing the company ~$1.1bn), buying out **TA Associates** (in since 2019) and part of the founder family. **Nova IVF** sits inside **Asia Healthcare Holdings (AHH)** — the TPG Growth-incubated, now **GIC-controlled** platform. **Ferty9** was bought to a **controlling stake by Verlinvest** ($50m, 2023). **Oasis Fertility** took $50m from **Kedaara** (minority, 2022). **Birla Fertility** is CK Birla group capital (domestic).
- **Mother & child** is dominated by AHH's **Motherhood Hospitals** (GIC/TPG control) and **Cloudnine** (Temasek + TPG NewQuest hold ~52%; a ~25%/₹11,000 cr stake sale to a global PE buyer was in process in mid-2026). **Rainbow Children's Medicare** is publicly listed but was built with **CDC/British International Investment** (UK DFI) backing.
- **Dialysis** is led by **NephroPlus** (Asia's largest; IPO Dec 2025; **Quadria Capital** ~30% largest shareholder; earlier capital from Investcorp, Bessemer, IFC, IIFL). **DCDC Kidney Care** is DFI/impact-funded (BII, ADB, IFU/Impact Fund Denmark, and Temasek-backed **ABC Impact**).
- **Oncology** ownership has shifted from PE to strategics: **American Oncology Institute/CTSI** went TPG Growth (2016) → **Varian** ($283m, 2019) → **Siemens Healthineers** (via Varian, 2021), with Siemens exploring an exit (2024–25). **HCG (HealthCare Global)** moved from **CVC** to **KKR** (up to 54% for up to $400m, announced Feb 2025).
- **Nephro-urology**: **AINU** is AHH-controlled (>70%, ₹600 cr, Sept 2023) — i.e., ultimately GIC/TPG.

**Ultimate foreign capital concentration:** US PE (TPG, General Atlantic, Bain/BPEA-EQT-Asia lineage, KKR, CVC [Lux/Channel Islands]), Singapore sovereign wealth (Temasek, GIC), Belgian family office (Verlinvest), Gulf (Investcorp, QIA — Qatar), and DFIs (IFC/World Bank, BII/UK, ADB, IFU/Denmark) recur across nearly every vertical. Common deal vehicles disclosed include **Singapore Pte Ltd** entities (Hyperion/Arvon/Claymore for Dr Agarwal's) and **Mauritius** (ChrysCapital; Claymore Investments (Mauritius)).

### 2.5 ONCOLOGY
