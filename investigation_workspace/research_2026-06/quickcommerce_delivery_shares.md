## 1. Overview / Executive Summary

India's online food-delivery and quick-commerce (q-commerce / 10-minute grocery) sectors are dominated by two listed holding companies and one large private challenger, all of which were built substantially on **foreign** venture, growth-equity, sovereign-wealth, pension and strategic capital:

- **Eternal Ltd (formerly Zomato Ltd)** — listed (NSE/BSE: ETERNAL). Owns the **Blinkit** quick-commerce business (acquired 2022). Historically backed by Alibaba's **Ant Group/Antfin (Singapore)**, plus US/global funds (Tiger Global, Temasek, Sequoia/Peak XV, Kora, etc.). As of late 2025 the company has **no promoter** and FIIs held roughly **36%**. Antfin fully **exited in August 2025**. The company is actively **capping foreign ownership at 49.5%** to retain "Indian-Owned and Controlled Company" (IOCC) status so Blinkit can run an inventory-led model.
- **Swiggy Ltd** — listed November 2024 (NSE/BSE: SWIGGY). Owns **Swiggy Instamart** (quick commerce). Pre-IPO it was ~**87.6% foreign-owned** (press-reported), dominated by **Prosus/Naspers (MIH, Netherlands)**, **SoftBank Vision Fund II**, **Accel**, **Tencent**, **Norwest (Mauritius)**, **GIC**, **Qatar Investment Authority**, **Coatue**, etc. As of early 2026 it remains majority-foreign and **failed** a May 2026 shareholder vote to amend its AoA toward IOCC status (72.36% vs 75% needed).
- **Zepto (Kiranakart Technologies Pvt Ltd)** — large private quick-commerce pure-play. Foreign-dominated cap table: **Nexus**, **Lightspeed**, **StepStone**, **Avenir**, **Glade Brook**, **General Catalyst**, **Y Combinator**, and — from October 2025 — **CalPERS** (California's public pension fund). **Re-domiciled from Singapore to India** (NCLT order, January 2025) ahead of a planned 2026 IPO; rapidly raising domestic capital to push Indian ownership from ~12–30% toward >50% for FDI/IOCC compliance.
- **BigBasket (Supermarket Grocery Supplies Pvt Ltd / Innovative Retail Concepts)** — controlled by **Tata Digital** since 2021 (CCI-approved 64.3% acquisition at ~$1.87bn valuation). The Tata deal gave **full exits** to prior foreign holders **Alibaba** (~25%) and **Actis** (~16.77%); **Mirae Asset** and **CDC Group** were earlier backers. Now effectively a Tata (Indian-promoter) asset.

**Market structure (analyst estimates):**
- **Quick commerce GMV** is concentrated. For 2025, **Datum Intelligence (via Reuters, Jan 2026)** put **Blinkit ~48%**, **Swiggy Instamart ~24%**, **Zepto ~22%** — implying a **CR3 of ~94%** among the three originals, with **Flipkart Minutes**, **BB Now (Tata)** and **Amazon** sharing the remainder. By Sept 2025 Blinkit was reported **>50%**.
- **Food delivery** is a near-**duopoly**: **Zomato/Eternal ~55–58%** vs **Swiggy ~42–45%** (analyst/press estimates), together **>90%** of the organized market — a **CR2 ≈ 100%** of the organized segment.
- **Quick-commerce market size:** roughly **$6–8bn GMV in 2024–FY25**, scaling toward **$25–40bn+ by 2030** depending on the analyst (Redseer / Bain / others). Walmart-owned **Flipkart** and **Amazon** entered aggressively in 2024–2026, intensifying competition.

The dominant ownership theme is a **transition from foreign to "Indian-owned and controlled"** status driven by FEMA/FDI rules that bar foreign capital from **inventory-led** e-commerce — pushing Eternal, Swiggy and Zepto to restructure ownership precisely because q-commerce economics favor an inventory model.

## 2. Detailed Findings

### 2.1 Eternal Ltd (formerly Zomato) — including Blinkit

**Corporate identity.** Zomato Ltd listed in July 2021; renamed **Eternal Ltd** in 2025. It is the holding company for the food-delivery brand **Zomato**, the quick-commerce brand **Blinkit**, going-out platform **District**, and B2B **Hyperpure**.

**Antfin / Ant Group / Alibaba history (foreign strategic capital):**
- **2018:** Ant Financial (Alibaba affiliate) invested ~**$210M for a 14.7%** stake, becoming Zomato's largest investor; raised to **~23%** by November 2018. *(press-reported)* — https://inc42.com/buzz/zomato-raises-150-mn-funding-at-3-bn-valuation-from-ant-financial/
- **Jan 2020:** Ant put in another **$150M** at a **$3bn** valuation, part of a broader ~$600M round. *(press-reported)* — https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/zomato-to-raise-150-million-from-investor-ant-financial-at-3-billion-valuation-241675-2020-01-10
- Post-IPO Ant/Antfin steadily trimmed; by mid-2025 **Antfin Singapore Holding Pte Ltd** held ~**1.9%**.
- **7 August 2025 — FULL EXIT:** Antfin Singapore sold its **entire ~1.9% (≈188.4 million shares)** via block deal at a **floor of ₹285** (~5% discount), raising **≈₹5,370 crore (~$612 million)**; **Morgan Stanley** was the reported broker. This was part of Antfin's broader unwinding of Indian consumer-tech (it had exited ~5% of Paytm days earlier). *(press-reported)* — https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/antfin-to-sell-entire-stake-in-eternal-formerly-zomato-via-block-deal-125080601672_1.html ; https://techstory.in/antfin-to-exit-zomato-parent-eternal-ltd-with-%E2%82%B95375-cr-block-deal/

**Blinkit (formerly Grofers) — origin and prior foreign backers:**
- Blinkit/Grofers had raised ~**$692M** from **SoftBank, DST Global, Tiger Global** among others; reached unicorn status after a $120M round from Zomato + Tiger Global. *(press-reported)* — https://www.indiatvnews.com/business/news/zomato-to-acquire-blinkit-rs-4447-crore-deal-blink-commerce-2022-06-24-787136
- **June 2022:** Zomato board approved acquisition of **Blink Commerce Pvt Ltd** for **₹4,447.48 crore** in an **all-stock** deal (62.85 crore new Zomato shares at ₹70.76), valuing Blinkit ~40% below its prior ~$1bn mark. The share-swap converted Blinkit's foreign VCs into Zomato (now Eternal) shareholders. *(VERIFIED via board-approval reporting; deal value from board resolution)* — https://mnacritique.mergersindia.com/news/blinkit-on-zomato-plate-board-oks-rs-4447cr-deal/

**Foreign ownership trajectory and the 49.5% cap (the central regulatory story):**
- FII holding fell from **~55.11% (Mar 2024)** → **54.11% (Jun 2024)** → **44.36% (Mar 2025)** → **36.24% (Dec 2025)** — a cumulative ~18.9 ppt decline over seven quarters. *(press-reported, citing exchange shareholding patterns)* — https://tradebrains.in/zomato-share-price-why-fiis-are-reducing-their-stake-in-eternal/
- **18 April 2025:** Eternal's board approved a special resolution to **cap total foreign ownership at 49.5%** (FDI+FPI+NRI, fully diluted) to **reinforce IOCC status**. At end-Mar 2025 foreign ownership was ~**44.88%**, below the cap. The explicit purpose: enable **Blinkit to shift from a marketplace model to an inventory-ownership model**, which FDI rules permit only for Indian-owned-and-controlled firms. *(VERIFIED — board resolution / company disclosure, press-reported)* — https://inc42.com/buzz/eternal-to-cap-foreign-ownership-at-under-50/ ; https://www.angelone.in/news/eternal-zomato-proposes-over-49-percent-cap-on-foreign-ownership-to-maintain-iocc-status
- **Current (FY26, Oct–Dec 2025 pattern):** **no promoter (0%)**; **FII ~36.24%**; **DII ~5.99% / 2.56%** (sources vary on the DII figure); retail ~31%. Foreign headroom rose above 25%, making the stock eligible for full MSCI weight (potential ~$390M passive inflow at the Feb review). *(press-reported, citing exchange filings)* — https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/zomato-parent-eternals-stock-jumps-over-4-as-shareholding-pattern-reveals-increased-foreign-headroom ; https://choiceindia.com/stocks/eternal-ltd-shareholding-pattern
- **Primary source for exact category breakdown:** Eternal IR shareholding page — https://www.eternal.com/investor-relations/governance/?tab=shareholding-pattern (use for the authoritative quarter-end figures).

### 2.4 BigBasket (Supermarket Grocery Supplies / Innovative Retail Concepts) — Tata Digital

**Prior foreign owners (pre-Tata):**
- **Early 2018:** **Alibaba** led a ~**$300M** round at ~$950M valuation; later **Alibaba, Mirae Asset Global Investments, and UK's CDC Group** backed a **$150M** Series F (May 2019) that made BigBasket a unicorn. *(press-reported)* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BigBasket
- Pre-acquisition, **Alibaba held ~25%** and **Actis ~16.77%**. *(press-reported)* — https://www.business-standard.com/economy/news/bigbasket-ceo-hari-menon-tata-group-quick-commerce-125090200292_1.html

**Tata Digital acquisition (the foreign-exit event):**
- **April 2021 — CCI approved** Tata Digital's acquisition of **64.3%** of **Supermarket Grocery Supplies Pvt Ltd** (BigBasket's parent), and SGS acquiring sole control of **Innovative Retail Concepts Pvt Ltd**. Reported price ~**$1.2bn** at ~**$1.87bn** valuation. The deal gave **full exits to Alibaba and Actis** — i.e., a transfer from Chinese/foreign hands to an **Indian promoter (Tata)**. *(VERIFIED — CCI clearance; deal value press-reported)* — https://inc42.com/buzz/bigbasket-board-approves-acquisition-by-tata-group/ ; https://www.tata.com/newsroom/business/tata-digital-bigbasket-acquisition
- **Subsequent:** Tata Digital invested **$550M+** more within ~18 months (incl. a **$200M** round ~Jan 2023 at a **~$3.2bn** valuation), deepening control. Founders (Hari Menon et al.) held ~5% and continued to run operations. *(press-reported)* — https://inc42.com/buzz/tata-digital-marks-4th-investment-in-bigbasket-worth-200-mn/ ; https://www.business-standard.com/economy/news/bigbasket-ceo-hari-menon-tata-group-quick-commerce-125090200292_1.html
- **Net:** BigBasket / BB Now is now an **Indian-promoter (Tata)** asset; foreign capital (Alibaba/Mirae/CDC/Actis) has substantially exited. The exact current Tata stake (whether still ~64% or higher after follow-on rounds) is **not cleanly disclosed** — see gaps.

## 5. Ownership Synthesis: the foreign-capital → "Indianization" arc

**STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION:** All four major franchises were built on foreign capital, and three of the four are now actively reducing or capping foreign ownership specifically to comply with India's FDI/FEMA architecture for e-commerce:

1. **FDI rule:** 100% foreign ownership is allowed in **marketplace** e-commerce but **prohibited in inventory-led** e-commerce; an inventory-led business must be **Indian-owned and controlled (IOCC, >50% resident Indian ownership)**. *(VERIFIED — established FDI policy; summarized by Business Standard)* — https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/startups-indian-controlled-status-swiggy-iocc-fema-rules-126051900528_1.html
2. Quick-commerce margins favor an **inventory/private-label** model → Eternal (Blinkit), Swiggy (Instamart) and Zepto all want IOCC status.
3. **Result:** Eternal capped foreign ownership at 49.5% (Apr 2025) and saw Antfin fully exit (Aug 2025); Zepto re-domiciled to India (Jan 2025) and is racing to >50% Indian ownership; Swiggy attempted (and as of May 2026 **failed**) the AoA amendment. BigBasket already solved this by becoming a Tata asset (foreign holders exited in 2021).

**EXPERT INTERPRETATION:** The Chinese-origin capital (Alibaba/Ant via Antfin, Tencent) has been the most aggressively unwound — Antfin's Eternal/Paytm exits and Alibaba's BigBasket exit reflect both regulatory pressure (Press Note 3 / China-border-state scrutiny since 2020) and geopolitics. **US/European pension and institutional money is, by contrast, still flowing IN** — most strikingly **CalPERS** leading Zepto's Oct 2025 round, and US/Dutch holders (Prosus, Coatue, StepStone, Glade Brook, Avenir, Lightspeed, Norwest, Accel) remaining heavily invested.
