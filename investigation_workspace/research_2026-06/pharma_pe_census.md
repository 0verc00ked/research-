## 1. Overview / Executive Summary

The Indian pharma services stack — APIs, CDMO/CRDMO and clinical CRO — has become one of the most heavily PE-owned segments of Indian healthcare, and the capital is overwhelmingly **foreign or foreign-pooled**. Three structural patterns recur:

1. **Foreign-controlled CDMO platforms built by US/European buyout funds.** Advent International (US) built the **Cohance Lifesciences** API/CDMO platform by buying control of RA Chem Pharma (2020), ZCL Chemicals (2021) and Avra Laboratories (2022), then acquired control of listed **Suven Pharmaceuticals** (2022–23, ~76.1% via a Cyprus vehicle, **Berhyanda Ltd**) and merged the two in 2025. **TPG (US)** controlled **Sai Life Sciences** from 2018 via a Singapore vehicle (**TPG Asia VII SF Pte Ltd**) until full exit in Aug 2025. A separate API platform, **Sekhmet Pharmaventures**, was built by **PAG (Hong Kong/Asia), CX Partners and Samara Capital** through Singapore holdco **Gamot API Pte Ltd**.

2. **Large foreign *minority* positions in founder-controlled listed pharma.** ChrysCapital (Mauritius-domiciled funds), Temasek (Singapore SWF), Capital International (US), Goldman Sachs and Quadria sit as significant minority holders in Intas, Mankind, Aragen and others — control remains with Indian founders (Jasti, Juneja/Bhatt, Reddy, Bakshi families) but a meaningful slice of economics and board influence is foreign.

3. **Sovereign-wealth and strategic capital into biosimilars.** Biocon Biologics drew Goldman Sachs (US), ADQ (Abu Dhabi SWF), True North (via Activ Pine), Tata Capital, Serum Institute and ultimately a ~12.9%+ strategic stake by **Viatris (US)** through the $3.335bn biosimilars acquisition (Nov 2022).

**Net read (`STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION`):** The CDMO/API/CRO sub-sector shows materially higher *foreign control* (not just minority FPI) than Indian branded-generics formulations, because buyout funds favour the export-oriented, dollar-revenue services model. The clearest foreign-*control* cases here are **Cohance/Suven (Advent, US)**, **Sai Life (TPG, US — now exited)** and **Sekhmet (PAG-led)**. Intas, Mankind, Eris, Neuland, Jubilant, Laurus and Piramal Pharma remain founder/Indian-controlled with foreign capital in *minority* roles.
