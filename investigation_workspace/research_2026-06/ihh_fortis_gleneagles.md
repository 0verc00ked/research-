## 1. Overview / Executive Summary

IHH Healthcare Berhad — a Malaysia-listed hospital operator that is itself controlled by **Japanese trading house Mitsui & Co. and the Malaysian sovereign-wealth fund Khazanah Nasional**, with the **Employees Provident Fund of Malaysia (EPF)** as a third large holder — controls the largest single foreign-CONTROL position in the Indian hospital sector. It holds this position through two distinct Indian platforms:

1. **Fortis Healthcare Limited** (BSE: 532843 / NSE: FORTIS) — a listed company in which IHH is the classified **promoter** holding **31.17%** (the single largest block; Fortis has no other promoter and a widely dispersed public float, so 31.17% is effective working control). IHH acquired this stake via a 2018 preferential allotment and finally completed its long-frozen mandatory open offer in **November 2025** after a roughly seven-year freeze ordered in the **Daiichi Sankyo contempt litigation** against Fortis's former promoters, the **Singh brothers (Malvinder and Shivinder)**.

2. **Gleneagles Healthcare India** (formerly Gleneagles Global Hospitals / Ravindranath GE Medical Associates, "RGE") — a chain IHH built up from a minority position and took to **100% ownership in 2023** for ~INR 740 crore, operating 6 hospitals / ~1,500 beds.

Combined, IHH's India network is reported at **~35 hospitals and over 5,000 beds across 11 states** (Fortis ≈ 27 facilities / ~4,750 beds; Gleneagles ≈ 6 hospitals / ~1,500 beds), with a publicly stated plan to add **~2,000 beds by 2028** (target ~7,000 beds). IHH has also tightened the corporate plumbing: in 2024 it began merging the Fortis and Gleneagles operating businesses (an O&M agreement under which Fortis runs five Gleneagles hospitals), and Fortis has bought back the private-equity stakes in its diagnostics arm **Agilus Diagnostics (the former SRL)**, raising Fortis's holding from ~57.7% to ~89.2% en route to full ownership.

The structure is significant for the investigation because it places a **Malaysian-listed, Japanese-/sovereign-fund-controlled** entity in working control of one of India's three largest private hospital networks and one of its largest diagnostics chains — a control chain that was contested through a multi-party 2018 bidding war (IHH vs. TPG-backed Manipal vs. Hero-Burman vs. KKR-backed Radiant vs. China's Fosun) and then frozen for seven years by the Supreme Court.

**Most important control facts (all VERIFIED FACT unless noted):**
- IHH ultimate owners: Mitsui ~32.7%, Khazanah ~26%, EPF ~11–13%. [STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED; press + filings]
- IHH stake in Fortis: **31.17%**, classified promoter. [VERIFIED FACT — company release, Nov 2025]
- Open offer (additional 26.1%) **completed Nov 2025 but drew near-zero acceptance** (1,778 shares, 0.0002%) because the Rs 170 offer price was ~6x below the ~Rs 980 market price. IHH did NOT reach 50%/majority via the offer. [VERIFIED FACT]
- IHH owns ~100% of Gleneagles India and ~62.7% of listed Fortis Malar (which has sold its hospital business). [VERIFIED FACT]
- The control was contested in the Daiichi/Singh-brothers fraud saga rooted in the 2008 Ranbaxy sale. [VERIFIED FACT]

## 3. The Daiichi Sankyo / Ranbaxy / Singh-brothers / Religare Saga (root of the seven-year freeze)

This is the legal backstory that froze IHH's open offer for ~7 years. It is essential context for the control chain.

**Timeline (VERIFIED FACT unless noted):**
- **June 2008:** Japanese drugmaker **Daiichi Sankyo acquired Ranbaxy Laboratories** from the Singh brothers (Malvinder & Shivinder Singh) under a Share Purchase/Subscription Agreement; the controlling-stake deal valued Ranbaxy at ~**USD 4.6 billion**. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/ranbaxy-daiichi-affair-how-why-the-deal-went-south-116081100270_1.html
- **2009 onward:** Daiichi alleged the Singhs concealed a "Self-Assessment Report" and the genesis/severity of US FDA / DOJ investigations into Ranbaxy data-integrity issues. Source: https://www.barandbench.com/columns/5-key-take-ways-from-ranbaxy-daiichi-dispute
- **2013:** Daiichi filed ICC arbitration in Singapore.
- **2016:** Singapore (ICC) tribunal ordered the Singh brothers to pay Daiichi ~**INR 3,500 crore** (≈ USD 500m+) for fraudulent misrepresentation/concealment. Source: https://www.bqprime.com/business/singh-brothers-must-pay-daiichi-rs-3500-crore-arbitral-award-rules-delhi-high-court
- **Delhi High Court** upheld enforcement of the ~INR 3,500-cr award against the Singh brothers and their companies (not their children). Source: https://www.bqprime.com/business/singh-brothers-must-pay-daiichi-rs-3500-crore-arbitral-award-rules-delhi-high-court
- During enforcement, Daiichi filed a **contempt plea** alleging the Singhs were dissipating/transferring the very Fortis shares pledged/relevant to satisfying the award — which is what dragged the IHH–Fortis transaction into the Supreme Court.

**Parallel Religare / RHC Holding fund-diversion case (VERIFIED FACT):**
- **March 2019:** Delhi Police Economic Offences Wing (EOW) registered an FIR against Malvinder Singh, Shivinder Singh, former Religare Enterprises CMD **Sunil Godhwani**, and N.K. Ghosal, on a complaint by **Religare Finvest Ltd (RFL)** alleging cheating of ~INR 700 crore (later quantified much higher). Source: https://www.business-standard.com/article/economy-policy/delhi-cops-register-case-against-fortis-singh-brothers-for-siphoning-funds-119032800970_1.html
- **2019:** Singh brothers arrested; sent to Tihar jail. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/former-religare-promoters-singh-brothers-sent-to-tihar-jail-till-october-31-119101700950_1.html
- **July 2022:** SEBI imposed an ~**INR 60 crore** penalty on the Singh brothers over the Religare Finvest fund re-routing. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/religare-finvest-fund-reroute-sebi-slaps-rs-60-cr-fine-on-singh-brothers-122072801457_1.html
- **January 2025:** A Delhi court **framed criminal conspiracy and cheating charges** against the Singh brothers, alleging siphoning of ~**INR 1,260 crore** from RFL (2016–2018), routed into entities including **RHC Holding** under the guise of loans. (Trial ongoing; no final conviction confirmed as of this dossier.) Source: https://theprint.in/india/religare-case-court-frames-conspiracy-cheating-charges-against-singh-brothers/2450977/ [Charges = VERIFIED FACT; guilt = UNPROVEN CLAIM pending trial]

**Significance (STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION):** The Singh brothers' fraud/diversion problems — rooted in the 2008 Ranbaxy sale to Daiichi and compounded by the Religare/RHC siphoning allegations — destroyed their grip on Fortis, created the distressed-control vacuum that triggered the 2018 bidding war, and (via Daiichi's contempt action) caused the Supreme Court freeze that delayed IHH's full consolidation for ~7 years.

## 4. The 2018 Bidding War for Fortis Control

After the Singhs lost control and Fortis faced a governance/liquidity crisis, multiple bidders competed for control in H1 2018. (Deal facts here are press-reported.)

**Bidders and key moves (press-reported):**
- **TPG-backed Manipal Health Enterprises** — initial agreement (late March 2018) to take Fortis's hospital business; criticized by minority shareholders as undervalued, which reopened the auction.
- **Hero Enterprise Investment Office + Burman Family Office** consortium — at one point (10 May 2018) selected by the board as the winner.
- **KKR-backed Radiant Life Care** (Abhay Soi).
- **China's Fosun International.**
- **IHH Healthcare Berhad** — ultimately accepted by the Fortis board (July 2018).
Source: https://www.franchiseindia.com/insights/en/article/malaysia-s-ihh-healthcare-beats-manipal-to-win-bidding-war-for-fortis.51211

**Winning structure (VERIFIED FACT, company/regulatory record):**
- IHH, via its indirect wholly-owned subsidiary **Northern TK Venture Pte Ltd (NTK, Singapore)**, subscribed to a **preferential allotment of 235,294,117 new Fortis shares at INR 170 per share — total ~INR 4,000 crore** (IHH described it as a RM 2.4 billion subscription) for a **~31.1% stake**, completed **November 2018**. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/seven-years-on-ihh-finally-gets-sebi-go-ahead-for-fortis-open-offer-125100301291_1.html ; https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/779155
- Under SEBI Takeover Regulations, acquiring >25% triggered a **mandatory open offer for an additional 26%/26.1%** of Fortis at INR 170/share (offer size ~INR 3,300–3,349 crore). This is the offer that was then frozen. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/seven-years-on-ihh-finally-gets-sebi-go-ahead-for-fortis-open-offer-125100301291_1.html
- **Vehicle/jurisdiction:** Northern TK Venture Pte Ltd is **Singapore-incorporated**, an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of IHH (Malaysia). [VERIFIED FACT]

## 5. The Supreme Court Freeze and the Seven-Year Delay (2018–2025)

- **December 2018:** Acting on Daiichi Sankyo's contempt plea, the **Supreme Court ordered status quo** on the sale of any controlling stake in Fortis, effectively **halting IHH's open offer**. IHH publicly announced its open offer would not proceed on the original timeline. Source: https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/ihh-healthcare-halts-open-offer-fortis-supreme-court-084440184--finance.html
- **April 2019:** Fortis pleaded with the Supreme Court to allow IHH to make the open offer (arguing minority shareholders were being penalized by the freeze). Source: https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/allow-ihh-healthcare-to-make-an-open-offer-fortis-plea-in-supreme-court-119041101179_1.html
- **22 September 2022:** Supreme Court **sentenced the Singh brothers to 6 months' jail** for contempt and **ordered a forensic audit** of the Fortis–IHH deal, while **declining at that point to lift the stay** on the open offer (the matter was effectively remanded / left subject to SEBI). This **22 Sept 2022 date later became the start date for the interest** payable to tendering shareholders. Source: https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/daiichi-fortis-case-sc-gives-six-month-jail-to-singh-brothers-declines-to-lift-stay-on-fortis-ihh-open-offer-347919-2022-09-22 ; https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/singh-brothers-awarded-6-months-jail-term-in-fortis-daiichi-sankyo-case-122092200372_1.html
- The stay was ultimately cleared/ the path opened, allowing SEBI to process the long-pending open offer in 2025. Source: https://www.hospitalmanagement.net/news/ihh-fortis-indian-supreme-court-lifts-stay/

## 6. 2025: SEBI Clearance and Completion of the Open Offer (the decisive recent development)

- **1–3 October 2025:** **SEBI cleared** IHH's stalled open offer, ~7 years after IHH first acquired ~31.1%. IHH **kept the offer price unchanged at INR 170/share** for the additional **26.1%** of Fortis, **plus interest of INR 53.80/share** (10% p.a. from the Supreme Court order dated 22 September 2022). At INR 170, the implied offer size was ~**INR 3,349 crore**; law-firm reporting characterized the overall transaction at ~**USD 490 million**. Sources: https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/seven-years-on-ihh-finally-gets-sebi-go-ahead-for-fortis-open-offer-125100301291_1.html ; https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/10/09/dcm138-biz-ihh-healthcare-fortis.html ; https://law.asia/saraf-sr-associates-ihh-healthcare-fortis-open-offer-490m/
- **Tender period: 20 October – 4 November 2025**, run in parallel with an open offer for **Fortis Malar Hospitals (26.11%)**. Source: https://www.minichart.com.sg/2025/10/16/ihh-healthcare-announces-timeline-for-fortis-and-malar-open-offers-tender-period-from-20-october-to-4-november-2025-12/
- **Result (announced 10 November 2025):** Acceptance was **negligible** — only **1,778 Fortis shares (0.0002%)** and **4,523 Malar shares (0.02%)** were tendered — because Fortis's market price (~**INR 980.10–980.4/share**, up >5x since 2018) hugely exceeded the INR 170 offer price. **IHH's holding therefore stayed at 31.17%** of Fortis (235,295,895 shares: 235,294,117 from the 2018 preferential allotment + 1,778 from the offer), and **62.73%** of Fortis Malar (11,756,925 shares). Sources: https://www.minichart.com.sg/2025/11/10/ihh-healthcare-berhad-announces-results-of-fortis-and-malar-open-offers-following-subscription-and-acquisition-transactions/ ; https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/779155

**Critical nuance (STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION):** Headlines in early/mid-2026 said "IHH commits to raise stake to 50%" and the open offer was *legally for* an additional 26.1% (which would have taken IHH to ~57%). In practice the offer **failed to add shares** because it was deeply out-of-the-money, so **IHH remains at ~31.17%** as a minority-but-controlling promoter. Any move to a true 50%+ majority would require a *future* market purchase, fresh preferential issue, or new offer at market price. Reports that IHH "committed to raise stake to 50%" describe **intent/strategy, not an executed majority** — treat "50% stake" as PLAUSIBLE HYPOTHESIS / forward statement, not a current fact. Sources: https://m.dailyhunt.in/news/india/english/business+upturn-epaper-bsnutme/fortis+healthcare+surges+nearly+3+as+ihh+commits+to+raise+stake+to+50+from+31-newsid-n707526517 ; https://www.digitalhealthnews.com/ihh-healthcare-increases-fortis-healthcare-stake-eyes-2-000-new-beds-by-2028

## 8. Fortis → Agilus Diagnostics (the former SRL): PE buyout to near-full ownership

Agilus Diagnostics Ltd (rebranded from **SRL Diagnostics / SRL Ltd**) is Fortis's diagnostics arm — one of India's largest diagnostics chains (4,000+ collection points; ~400+ labs).

- Fortis began at **~57.68%** of Agilus and moved to buy out the PE investors. Source: https://www.bwhealthcareworld.com/article/cci-approves-fortis-healthcare%E2%80%99s-stake-hike-in-agilus-diagnostics-and-db-power-merger-537853
- **August 2024:** Fortis agreed to acquire the remaining **~31.52%** held by PE investors — **International Finance Corporation (IFC, World Bank Group), NYLIM Jacob Ballas India Fund III (managed by Jacob Ballas Capital), and Resurgence PE Investments (managed by Avigo Capital Partners)** — for ~**INR 1,780 crore**, valuing Agilus at ~**INR 5,700 crore** (≈ USD 0.69 bn). Sources: https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/fortis-set-to-buy-back-pe-stake-in-diagnostic-arm-agilus-for-rs-1-780-crore-124080801553_1.html ; https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/FORTIS-HEALTHCARE-LIMITED-9059727/news/Fortis-Healthcare-Limited-completed-the-acquisition-of-remaining-31-52-stake-in-Agilus-Diagnostics-48808999/
- **29 October 2024:** **CCI approved** Fortis's increased stake in Agilus. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/fortis-healthcare-gets-cci-nod-to-acquire-stake-in-agilus-diagnostics-124102901329_1.html
- **20 December 2024:** Fortis acquired **7.61% (5,970,149 shares) from IFC for ~INR 429.37 crore**, lifting Fortis to **89.2%**, en route to **100%**. Source: https://www.angelone.in/news/share-market/fortis-healthcare-acquires-stake-in-agilus-diagnostics-from-ifc

**Significance (STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION):** This is a reverse flow vs. the rest of this dossier — here **foreign/development PE (IFC) and India-focused PE funds exited** a major diagnostics asset back to the operating company (now IHH-controlled). Net effect: a large Indian diagnostics chain consolidating under the IHH→Fortis umbrella, removing IFC/Jacob Ballas/Avigo from the cap table.
