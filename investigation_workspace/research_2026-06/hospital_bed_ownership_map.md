# Consolidated PE / Sovereign / Institutional Hospital Bed-Count & Ownership Map (India)

**Research cell:** Consolidated PE/sovereign hospital bed-count + ownership table (for concentration)
**Compiled:** 2026-06-24
**Purpose:** Build the single missing artifact — a bed-level ownership-tagged table of India's largest organized corporate hospital chains — to directly test the repository claim that *"PE-controlled hospital beds reached ~25% of India's organized hospital capacity"* (repository claim RC003 / L02 / L03).
**Author note on method:** All bed counts and revenue figures below were taken, wherever possible, from the chain's own FY25/FY26 investor presentation, IPO/DRHP document, or annual report (downloaded and text-extracted), or from official CCI / PIB / stock-exchange (BSE/NSE) filings. Press-reported deal facts are explicitly labelled. Every numeric claim carries a source URL and an evidence classification.

---

## 1. Overview and headline conclusions

This dossier maps **14 of the largest organized corporate hospital platforms in India** by latest verified bed count, approximate annual revenue, and ownership classification. The central methodological lesson is that **the "25% PE-controlled" claim is entirely denominator-dependent**, and depending on which denominator is used the answer ranges from ~1–3% (all-India beds) to ~36–57% (narrow organized large-chain beds).

**Headline (the 14-chain organized sample = ~69,700 beds, mixed capacity/operational basis):**

- **Foreign-PE controlled (Blackstone, KKR):** ~13,125 beds = **18.8%** of the sample. — STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION (control verified by filings; bed totals on a mixed capacity/operational basis).
- **Foreign-sovereign/pension controlled (Temasek-backed Manipal):** ~12,367 beds = **17.7%** of the sample.
- **Foreign PE + sovereign combined:** ~25,492 beds = **36.6%** of the sample.
- **All foreign-controlled (PE + sovereign + strategic IHH):** ~31,742 beds = **45.6%** of the sample.
- **Domestic / listed-dispersed / family-controlled:** ~37,934 beds = **54.4%** of the sample.

**Concentration of the organized corporate-chain bed pool (within the 14-chain sample):**
- **CR4** (Manipal, Aster-QCIL, Apollo, Narayana) ≈ **56.2%** of sample beds.
- **CR8** (add KIMS, Max, Fortis, Medanta) ≈ **82.7%** of sample beds.

**Against all-India denominators the same platforms are small:**
- All-India total-bed proxy ≈ **1.886 million** beds (NITI/Knight Frank density 1.3/1,000 × World Bank 2024 population 1.4509 bn). The 14-chain sample = **~3.7%**; foreign-controlled subset = **~1.7%**; foreign-PE+sovereign = **~1.35%**.
- Implied private-bed proxy ≈ **1.122 million** beds (ICRA: private = ~59–60% of beds). Sample = **~6.2%**; foreign-controlled = **~2.8%**; foreign-PE+sovereign = **~2.3%**.
- Official government/NHM facility beds = **818,661** (PHC+CHC+SDH+DH+Medical Colleges, as on 31.03.2023) — a *separate, official* denominator that does NOT capture private or non-NHM beds.

**Verdict on the repository's "25% of organized beds PE-controlled" claim:**
**PARTIALLY VERIFIED / DENOMINATOR-DEPENDENT (EXPERT INTERPRETATION).** Under a *narrow organized large-chain denominator* (ICRA's 13–18 large chains, ~56,000–71,000 existing beds), foreign-PE+sovereign control of beds is plausibly in the **35–46%** range and *all* foreign-controlled beds (including strategic IHH) ~**45–57%** — i.e. the 25% figure is, if anything, *conservative* on this denominator. Under an *all-India* or *all-private* denominator, PE/sovereign-controlled beds are only **~1.3–3%**. The claim should never be quoted without stating the denominator. There is no public ownership-tagged bed census of *all* organized private beds in India; the figures here are a defensible lower-bound enumeration of the largest known platforms, not an exhaustive census.

> **Denominator caveat (critical):** These denominators are not on a like-for-like basis. The 818,661 official figure is *government NHM facilities only*. The 1.886M and 1.122M are *derived proxies*. The ICRA organized-chain figures (~56k–71k) are the only denominators on the same conceptual basis as the chain bed counts below, and are the correct denominator for an "organized corporate beds" concentration ratio. **The "organized" universe is not officially defined and no regulator publishes an ownership-tagged private-bed census.**

> **Bed-basis caveat:** Chains report on different bases — *capacity/licensed beds* vs *operational beds* vs *census beds*. Apollo reports 10,187 capacity beds but only 9,458 operational census beds; KIMS reports 5,179 capacity vs 4,492 operational; Shalby 2,297 capacity vs 1,415 operational. The sample total below mixes capacity and operational figures (each row notes its basis). A strict like-for-like operational-bed total would be materially lower. **Concentration shares are therefore approximate.**

| ICRA 18-chain organized | ~36% |

**Interpretation (EXPERT INTERPRETATION):** Organized corporate hospital beds are *highly concentrated* (CR8 ≈ 83% of the largest-chain pool), and within that organized pool, foreign capital — PE (Blackstone, KKR), sovereign (Temasek), and strategic (IHH) — controls roughly **45–57%** of beds depending on denominator, with PE+sovereign alone ~**36–46%**. The repository's "25% PE-controlled" figure is therefore *plausible and arguably conservative* **only** on the organized-large-chain denominator. On any all-India or all-private denominator the share collapses to **1–3%**, because organized chains are themselves a small slice (~4–6%) of all Indian beds — the Indian hospital bed stock remains dominated by government facilities and a vast fragmented standalone/small-nursing-home private sector.

## 8. Gaps and unverified items

1. **Bed-basis inconsistency (material):** Apollo, KIMS, Aster, Max report *capacity/licensed* beds; Narayana, Fortis report *operational*; Shalby/Jupiter distinguish operational vs capacity sharply (Shalby 1,415 operational vs 2,297 capacity). The sample total mixes bases and slightly overstates true operational beds. A clean operational-only total would likely be ~10–15% lower (~58k–62k). *Needs normalization to a single basis.*
2. **No exhaustive organized-bed census:** The 14 chains are the largest *known* platforms, not all organized private beds. Mid-size and regional chains (e.g., Sterling/Arpwood-OTPP, Asian/AHH-GIC-TPG, Sahyadri pre-merger, Cygnus, Ujala Cygnus, Marengo/CIMS, AIG, Sparsh, Care/CARE standalone where not in QCIL, GEMS, etc.) are not separately enumerated here. Adding them would *raise* the foreign-PE/sovereign numerator and the organized denominator — net effect on share is unknown without the full list.
3. **Manipal revenue & exact Temasek %:** Manipal is unlisted; FY25 revenue and the precise current Temasek/Sheares/Mubadala/Manipal-Group cap table are not in a public filing reviewed here (the 41% Temasek step and ~59% group control are company-release/press). The DRHP (S117) confirms beds and promoter list but the abridged version reviewed did not yield a clean consolidated revenue figure.
4. **Aster-QCIL legal completion pending:** Merger structure is verified but NCLT sanction/effective date was still pending (hearing fixed 22 May 2026 per repo S118). Combined 10,625-bed entity and the 30.7% Blackstone stake are *announced*, not yet legally effective at time of writing.
5. **Fortis/IHH control %:** "~31.5%" and "de-facto control" are press-reported; the exact current Northern TK Venture stake and any creeping-acquisition updates should be pulled from Fortis's latest BSE shareholding pattern (not captured here due to a 403 on the company investor page).
6. **Medanta, HCG, Yatharth, Rainbow, Jupiter, Shalby revenues** are partly press/analyst-derived for FY25 and should be replaced with audited annual-report figures.
7. **Sterling Hospitals (Arpwood/OTPP full ownership)** is press/ICRA-reported only and not filing-verified in this dossier.
8. **Denominator reconciliation:** The 818,661 official figure (govt NHM facilities only), the ~1.886M derived total proxy, and the ICRA ~56k–71k organized-chain figures are on three different conceptual bases. A true "organized private beds" denominator (all corporate/chain private beds, ownership-tagged) does not exist publicly and would require an MCA + state Clinical-Establishment-Act facility-level build. This is the single biggest open gap for closing the "25%" debate definitively.
9. **WebFetch/curl access limits:** Apollo and Fortis investor pages returned HTTP 403 to WebFetch; figures were recovered via direct curl PDF download (Apollo) or press/PR (Fortis Q4FY25 release). ICRA July-2025 PR is subscription-styled but the open-media PDF was extracted locally.
