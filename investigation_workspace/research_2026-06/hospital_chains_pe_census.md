## 1. Overview

The Indian corporate hospital sector splits into four ownership archetypes, and this census tags each chain accordingly:

1. **Foreign-strategic controlled / largest-shareholder** — Fortis (IHH Healthcare, Malaysia/Khazanah sovereign-linked).
2. **Foreign PE jointly-controlled (live deal)** — Aster DM Healthcare merging into "Aster DM Quality Care," to be co-controlled by Blackstone (US PE). Quality Care India (CARE Hospitals + KIMSHEALTH) was itself a Blackstone/TPG asset.
3. **Listed-dispersed, founder-led with very high FPI float** — Apollo Hospitals (FPI ~43%, but founder Reddy family is promoter), Medanta/Global Health (Temasek ~17% via Mauritius; Carlyle fully exited), KIMS Hyderabad (General Atlantic fully exited; now broad institutional), Rainbow Children's Medicare (CDC/BII exited; ADIA and other FPIs present).
4. **Founder / family controlled with modest FPI** — Narayana Hrudayalaya (Shetty family ~63%; early PE JP Morgan/Ambadevi exited at 2015 IPO), Yatharth, Shalby, Jupiter Life Line, Kovai Medical (KMCH).
5. **Non-profit charitable trust (no equity owners)** — Aravind Eye Care System (GOVEL Trust).

**Key cross-cutting finding:** Several marquee US/global PE entries into Indian hospitals have already been *fully harvested via IPO or block deals* (Carlyle out of Medanta, General Atlantic out of KIMS, CDC/British International Investment out of Rainbow). The persistent foreign presence today is (a) **IHH** as a strategic controller of Fortis, (b) **Blackstone** as an incoming joint-controller via Aster–Quality Care, and (c) **diffuse FPI float** (sovereign wealth such as ADIA/Temasek, US asset managers) sitting in the listed names rather than concentrated control blocks.

**Caveat on "FPI %":** The screener.in / exchange "FIIs/FPI" line aggregates *all* foreign portfolio investors (sovereign wealth, pension, asset managers, hedge funds). It is a VERIFIED FACT as a category total (sourced to the quarterly exchange shareholding filing), but it does NOT by itself identify any single foreign owner or imply control. Where a single foreign holder's stake is cited, it is separately sourced.

### 2.2 Fortis Healthcare Ltd (NSE: FORTIS) — IHH Healthcare controlled
- **Ownership type:** **Foreign-strategic largest-shareholder controlled.** IHH Healthcare Berhad (Malaysia; major shareholder Khazanah Nasional — Malaysian sovereign wealth; also Mitsui) is the single largest shareholder.
- **Latest shareholding (Mar 2026):** Promoters (IHH/NTK Ventures) **31.17%**; FIIs/FPI **25.98%**; DIIs **31.35%**; Public **11.50%**. *(VERIFIED FACT — exchange filing aggregated)* — https://www.screener.in/company/FORTIS/consolidated/

Post-offer (confirmed by BSE 7 Nov 2025) IHH/NTK holds **235,295,895 Fortis shares = 31.17%** — i.e., effectively unchanged. IHH separately holds **62.73%** of Fortis Malar Hospitals. IHH has stated *intent* to reach 50% but has not done so. *(VERIFIED FACT — exchange-confirmed offer result)* — https://www.minichart.com.sg/2025/11/10/ihh-healthcare-berhad-announces-results-of-fortis-and-malar-open-offers-following-subscription-and-acquisition-transactions/

- **Foreign-control assessment:** Fortis is **the clearest case of foreign strategic control by stake** in this census — IHH (Malaysia/Khazanah sovereign-linked) is single largest shareholder and classified as promoter at 31.17%, with declared intent to consolidate to 50%. *(STRONG EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION)*
